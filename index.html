Straight answers

Before we connect anything.

Does Arvinify automate LinkedIn activity?

Not with scraping, mass DMs, account sharing or unattended bots. Arvinify builds the conversion system around LinkedIn and keeps content and outreach human-approved.

Do we have to replace our website or CRM?

Usually not. The pilot starts with one source you already use and connects only what the approved workflow requires.

Do you guarantee leads or revenue?

No. LinkedIn reach and buyer behaviour cannot be guaranteed. The pilot guarantees only the written scope we agree to build and the measurement needed to judge it.

Can you serve US companies?

Yes. The pilot is available to eligible B2B service firms in both the United States and Canada, with online setup payment in USD or CAD.

How quickly can the pilot go live?

The target is within 10 business days after access, content and written approval are provided. The confirmed schedule is included in the written scope.

Do we need a sales call to start?

No. Fit check, scoping, approval and delivery can run through the written brief and email. A phone number is not required.

Can we pay online?

Yes. The fixed setup can be paid through secure Stripe-hosted checkout. Monthly monitoring is activated separately after written go-live approval.
