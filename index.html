The revenue leak

Good leads disappear in the gaps between tools.

Most service firms do not need another chatbot. They need a reliable operating layer between the moment a prospect raises a hand and the moment a person takes over.

01 / SPEED

First replies arrive too late

High-intent prospects move on when the first useful response waits for a busy inbox.

02 / CONTEXT

Lead details are incomplete

Teams lose time asking the same questions or chasing information that could be collected upfront.

03 / MOMENTUM

Follow-up stops too soon

Qualified opportunities go quiet because reminders and next steps rely on memory.

04 / HANDOFF

Systems fall out of sync

Forms, calendars and CRMs tell different stories, leaving ownership unclear.

Straight answers

Before we connect anything.

Is this just a chatbot?

No. Chat can be one input, but the product is the workflow around it: capture, response, qualification, routing, configured follow-up, booking, CRM handoff and human review.

Do we have to replace our CRM or website?

Usually not. The goal is to connect the tools your team already relies on and replace only what is actively blocking the workflow.

Will AI contact leads without limits?

No. Every channel is configured deliberately with consent, timing and escalation rules, plus a clear point where a person takes over.

Do we need a sales call to start?

No. Intake, scoping, approval and delivery can all run asynchronously through the written brief and email. No meeting is required.

Can you still fix the underlying website?

Yes. Website Rescue remains available for urgent technical issues and can be combined with an automation build when the conversion path itself needs repair.
