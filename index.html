Straight answers

Questions before you send us your site.

What kinds of websites do you work on?+
Shopify, WordPress, and custom sites built with React, Next.js and other modern frontend stacks, plus common builders like Webflow, Wix and Squarespace. If it runs in a browser and something is broken, it's usually in scope.
How fast can you fix my website?+
Many small, clearly scoped repairs are completed within 24 hours after access and scope are confirmed. Complexity varies, so more involved issues take longer, and we always tell you the realistic timeline before larger work begins.
What does the CAD $249 Website Rescue include?+
Diagnosis of the issue, a targeted repair of a small problem or a few closely related ones, responsive verification, a basic regression check on the affected area, and a short completion summary so you know exactly what changed.
Do I need to rebuild my website?+
Usually no. Website Rescue is intentionally focused on targeted repairs. We fix what's broken and leave the rest of your site alone unless you specifically ask for more.
What access will you need?+
It depends on the platform. Shopify usually needs collaborator access, WordPress needs admin or hosting access, and a custom codebase needs repository access. We request only what the specific fix requires, nothing more.
Can you fix Shopify?+
Yes. Product page issues, cart and checkout problems, theme and Liquid glitches, and other frontend ecommerce problems are core to what we do.
Can you work with React or Next.js?+
Yes. We debug and apply targeted production fixes to React and Next.js frontends without rewriting your application.
What if the problem is bigger than expected?+
We reproduce the issue first. If the fix is larger than a standard rescue, you get a clear scope and price to approve before we do any additional work. You're never billed for scope you didn't agree to.
