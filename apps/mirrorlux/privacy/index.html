MirrorLux Privacy Policy

MirrorLux was built so that using it doesn't require giving anything up. This page explains exactly what that means.

Last updated: August 2026

Summary

MirrorLux does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data. It does not require an account. It has no analytics, no advertising identifiers, and no third-party trackers. The camera feed is used only to display your live reflection on screen, it is never recorded, saved, or sent anywhere.

Camera access

MirrorLux requires access to your device's camera to function, since the app is a mirror and needs a live image to display. This feed is processed entirely on your device, in real time, to render Edge Light, Smooth Zoom, Mirror Mode, and Freeze Frame. No image or video data is ever uploaded, transmitted, or stored by MirrorLux, including while using Freeze Frame, which holds a frame temporarily on-screen and discards it when you exit the app or unfreeze.

Accounts and personal information

MirrorLux does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number in order to use any feature.

Data we do not collect

  • Photos, video, or camera data
  • Account or profile information
  • Location data
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Usage analytics or behavioral tracking

Third parties

MirrorLux does not share data with third parties, because it does not collect data to share. MirrorLux does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking software development kits.

Children's privacy

Because MirrorLux collects no personal data from any user, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, for example if a future version of MirrorLux introduces an optional feature that involves data collection, this page will be updated and the "last updated" date above will reflect the change.

Contact us

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@arvinify.com.
