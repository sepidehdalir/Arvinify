FAQ
Questions people actually ask.
How is MirrorLux different from the Camera app?+

The Camera app is built for taking photos. MirrorLux is a makeup mirror app built for looking at yourself in real time, with edge lighting, zoom, and mirroring tuned for close-up work instead of photography, with no shutter, no camera roll, and no photo library involved.

Does MirrorLux upload my photos?+

MirrorLux doesn't take or store photos at all during normal mirror use, and nothing is ever uploaded anywhere. What the camera sees stays on your device, in real time, and is never sent to a server.

Can I use MirrorLux without an internet connection?+

Yes. MirrorLux works entirely offline. As a portable mirror, it needs to work in a bathroom, a car, or backstage with no signal, so it never requires a connection to function.

Does MirrorLux work well in dark rooms?+

Yes, that's what Edge Light is for. It lights your face from the screen's edges like a mirror with light built in, so you can see clearly even in dim rooms or during early morning and late night routines.

Can I zoom in while Edge Light is on?+

Yes. Smooth Zoom and Edge Light work together, so you can get a closer look at a specific area while staying fully lit, useful for precise eyeliner, brows, or spot-checking skin.

Is MirrorLux a good vanity mirror replacement?+

For most everyday routines, yes. As a vanity mirror replacement, MirrorLux adds adjustable lighting and zoom a fixed mirror can't offer, and it travels with you instead of staying on a countertop.

Does MirrorLux work as a grooming mirror for shaving?+

Yes. As a grooming mirror, MirrorLux's zoom and edge lighting are just as useful for shaving, trimming, and skincare as they are for makeup application.

Can I use MirrorLux to check my contact lenses?+

Yes. As a contact lens mirror, Smooth Zoom and even, shadow-free Edge Light make it easier to see exactly what you're doing during insertion and removal.

Is MirrorLux a subscription app?+

No. MirrorLux has no subscriptions of any kind.

Do I need to create an account to use MirrorLux?+

No account, no sign-in, and no personal information required. Open the app and it's ready.

What makes MirrorLux a good beauty mirror for everyday use?+

As a beauty and cosmetic mirror, it combines the three things a real vanity mirror offers, good lighting, magnification, and a stable reflection, in a device you already carry everywhere.

Can MirrorLux be used as a digital makeup light?+

Yes. Edge Light is designed specifically as a makeup light: warm, neutral, and cool tones let you preview how your look will read under different lighting conditions before you leave the house.

Does MirrorLux work as a selfie mirror in bright light?+

Yes. Cool tone Edge Light and Smooth Zoom hold up well outdoors or under harsh overhead lighting, so it works as a reliable selfie mirror in bright conditions too.

Which iPhone models does MirrorLux support?+

MirrorLux supports current iPhone models running a supported version of iOS. Exact compatibility is listed on the App Store product page before you download.
