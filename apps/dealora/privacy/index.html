Effective September 23, 2026 · Dealora for iPhone, version 1.0

No data collected by the app

Dealora does not collect, transmit, sell or track personal data. The app has no account system, advertising, analytics SDK, backend or location access. All calculations work on your device.

Saved calculations and preferences

Saved calculations, currency choice and the default sales-tax rate are stored locally. Your Apple device-backup settings may include this data. You can delete saved entries in the app. Deleting the app removes its local data, subject to backup and restore behavior controlled by Apple and your settings.

Sharing and external links

When you choose Share, iOS sends the summary to the destination you select. That service applies its own privacy practices. Opening support or privacy links takes you to the Arvinify website; normal website hosting may process technical connection data such as your IP address.

Support messages

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you send so we can respond. This is separate from app usage; please do not send sensitive information.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@arvinify.com.
