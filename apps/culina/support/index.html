Help for Culina: Cooking Converter on iPhone, version 1.0.

Convert a measurement

Enter a decimal or fraction, then choose From and To units. For volume and weight, select the ingredient and preparation that match your recipe. Density changes with packing, moisture and brand.

Scale a recipe

Set the original and desired servings. Add ingredients with their original amounts, unit and name. Tap a row to edit; swipe to delete. Results update automatically. Cooking time and pan size may need separate adjustments.

Choose the right regional unit

A US cup is approximately 236.59 ml; a metric cup is 250 ml. Australian tablespoons are 20 ml, while metric/UK tablespoons are 15 ml. Fluid ounces measure volume; ounces measure weight.

Favorites and recent conversions

Tap Save on a conversion to favorite it. Recent results are saved automatically. Open Saved to share or delete entries. Data is stored locally; deleting the app removes its local data.

Oven guidance

Fan guidance subtracts 20°C from a conventional recipe. Do not apply this again if the recipe already states a fan setting. Gas marks are approximate; follow your appliance manual.

Contact support

Email support@arvinify.com. Include your iOS version, Culina version and a description of the issue. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information.
