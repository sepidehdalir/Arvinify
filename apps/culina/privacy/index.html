Effective September 23, 2026 · Culina for iPhone, version 1.0

No data collected by the app

Culina does not collect, transmit, sell or track personal data. It has no account system, advertising, analytics SDK, backend, subscriptions or in-app purchases. Calculations and ingredient references work offline.

Local storage

Recipes, favorites, recent conversions and the introduction preference are stored locally on your device. Your operating system backup settings may include this data. You can delete saved entries in the app; deleting the app removes its local data.

Sharing and external links

Copy and Share operate only when you choose them. The destination you select receives the shared result and handles it under its own privacy practices. Reference and support links open websites outside Culina.

Support and this website

If you email support, we receive the information you voluntarily send and use it to respond. The website host and email provider may process technical information needed to deliver their services. This does not change the app’s no-data-collection behavior.

Children and changes

The app collects no personal data from users of any age. This policy will be updated if a future version changes how data is handled.
