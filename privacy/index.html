Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains what information Arvinify collects when you use this website, submit a LinkedIn-to-revenue fit check, purchase a pilot, request Website Rescue or contact us.

What we collect

We collect the information you choose to send and limited operational information needed to deliver and secure the service. That typically includes:

  • Your company name, website and the United States or Canada market you select
  • LinkedIn profile or company-page links and information about your buyer, offer and sales path
  • Workflow, lead-volume, client-value and timing information entered in the fit check
  • Your name and work email address; the LinkedIn pilot does not require a phone number
  • Billing contact, transaction status and related checkout metadata supplied by Stripe
  • Messages, screenshots or project details you later choose to email us

Our server also processes ordinary security data such as request time, IP address and browser headers for abuse prevention and troubleshooting. We do not sell personal information or run cross-site advertising trackers.

How we use it

We use your information to assess fit, recommend and agree a scope, create checkout, confirm payment, deliver the approved implementation, send operational follow-up and measure the agreed pilot. We do not use it for unrelated marketing without your consent.

Account and project access

We request only the access the approved work needs. We do not ask you to share a LinkedIn password. Where website, hosting, analytics, email or code access is needed, use revocable, least-privilege access where available and remove it when the work is complete.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We use service providers to operate the service, including Vercel for hosting and server functions, Resend for transactional email and Stripe for checkout and payment processing. Each receives only the information needed for its role. Stripe collects card and payment details under its own privacy terms; Arvinify does not receive full card numbers.

Retention

We keep request, payment-status and project information for as long as needed to provide the service, maintain security and meet ordinary accounting, tax and legal requirements, then delete or de-identify it when it is no longer needed.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing the address below. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Cookies

This site does not set advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. If we add basic, privacy-respecting analytics in the future, we will update this policy first.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@arvinify.com.
