Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Arvinify Technology Inc. handles information on arvinify.com. Individual apps may include a product-specific privacy policy linked from their App Store page.

Website information

This studio website does not require an account. We receive personal information only when you choose to email us, such as your name, email address and the content of your message.

App privacy

Our apps are designed to minimise data collection. Many products work locally on your device and do not require an account. Because features differ, the policy for each app is the authoritative description of its camera, photo, journal, purchase or other data handling.

How we use contact information

We use messages to answer support questions, investigate technical issues and respond to legitimate business enquiries. We do not sell personal information.

Service providers

Our website is hosted by Vercel. Email is handled by our email provider. Apple processes App Store downloads and purchases under Apple’s own terms and privacy practices.

Retention and choices

We retain support correspondence only as long as reasonably needed for support, legal and security purposes. You may request access, correction or deletion of information you sent to us, subject to legal obligations.
